LeadGen AI

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

LeadGen AI collects information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and LinkedIn profile data when you connect your LinkedIn account via OAuth. We also collect lead data you import or create within the application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the LeadGen AI service
  • Generate AI-powered outreach messages and lead scoring
  • Sync data between your connected integrations (LinkedIn, CRM, etc.)
  • Improve our service and user experience

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely in our Supabase database with row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring only you can access your data. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit.

4. LinkedIn Data Usage

When you connect your LinkedIn account, we access your basic profile information (name, email, headline) via the LinkedIn OpenID Connect API. We do not access your LinkedIn connections, messages, or other private data. LinkedIn data is used solely to enhance lead management within the application.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only shared with the services you explicitly connect (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.) at your request.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings page.
